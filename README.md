# Senior Trade Analyst | Process Optimization & Business Automation

Hello — I'm a Senior Trade Analyst with more than seven years of experience in customs and global trade.

My work sits at the intersection of process knowledge and practical automation. I focus on understanding how business workflows operate, identifying repeatable logic and controls, and building solutions that make day-to-day work clearer, faster, and easier to manage.

I am developing further in Process Automation, AI Adoption, and Digital Transformation. My profile is business-side automation: the process comes first, and technology is used where it creates practical value.

## How I approach automation

> Automate repeatable logic, surface exceptions, retain human judgement.

The goal is not to remove people from a process. It is to handle consistent, rules-based work efficiently while making exceptions visible and leaving context-dependent decisions with the people best placed to make them.
