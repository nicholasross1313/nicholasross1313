# Practical Process Automation for Small Teams

I help small operations teams remove repetitive office work from Excel, email, PDFs and browser-based workflows.

The focus is simple: find a task that is repeated every day or every week, measure the time it takes, and automate the repeatable part without turning it into a large IT project.

## Good fit

Typical examples include:

- copying data from PDF documents into Excel
- saving and renaming email attachments
- comparing two reports and highlighting exceptions
- preparing recurring daily or weekly reports
- reconciling data between spreadsheets
- merging, splitting or renaming batches of documents
- browser-based repetitive tasks
- AI-assisted document extraction with human review

## Selected results from professional automation work

These examples are anonymised and contain no employer or client data.

### Operations follow-up
A repetitive monitoring and follow-up process was reduced from roughly **4.5 hours per day to 1.5 hours per day**.

### Document handling
A workflow processing hundreds of documents per month reduced manual handling from roughly **3 minutes per document to around 30 seconds**.

### Report download workflow
A recurring download task was reduced from roughly **45 minutes per day to around 3.5 minutes**.

### Spreadsheet reconciliation
Large, rules-based reconciliation tasks that previously required days of manual work were reduced to hours using structured automation and exception handling.

## How I work

1. You show me the current process.
2. We define the exact input and expected output.
3. I estimate the time-saving and confirm whether automation is worth doing.
4. We agree a fixed scope and price.
5. I build and test the solution.
6. You receive the tool, a short guide and a support period.

If a process is too variable or automation does not make economic sense, I will say so.
